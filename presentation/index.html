04 / 15
05 · POV

The spine of every decision

Engagement · Narrative · Return

Engagement
Every screen invites the user somewhere next.
CTAs on every card lead to deeper screens. No surface is terminal. The user always has a next step.
Narrative
Every stat tells a story.
Position-aware stats wrapped in human-language context. Numbers earn meaning. "Joint top scorer" beats "9 goals".
Return
Every story is a reason to come back.
The home screen surfaces something new every visit — mid-week and match-week alike.

This is the spine of every design decision in this submission. The rest of the deck is evidence.

14 · Open questions

What I assumed · what I'd validate

Things I assumed. Things I'd validate.

01
League-wide comparisons require querying across teams.
Is this in the current data model, or would it need to be built?
02
Match-by-match data is assumed available.
Confirms with current backend infrastructure.
03
Fact generation logic — rule-based, LLM-assisted, or hybrid?
Affects how narratives scale across players and weeks.
04
PRO gating: hero narratives stay free, deep stats stay gated?
Or does the narrative layer become its own paid tier?

I made these assumptions to keep moving. Each one is a real conversation.
